## AI inside the product: the "Ask the data" assistant (text for the submission field "Use of Artificial Intelligence")

The dashboard contains one AI component, the optional "Ask the data" page. A question is matched (BM25 keyword search, no AI)
against two sources only: (1) our own verified results - the ranked insights, validation metrics, scenario-model numbers
and the PSI investigation catalog, all generated by the project's code from NASA PSI data - and (2) excerpts of 12 public
NASA technical reports from NTRS. The retrieved excerpts and the question are sent to Google Gemini (free tier), which
is instructed to answer only from them, to cite the source block after every statement and to say when the data do not
cover the question. The app checks every citation against the excerpts it sent, removes unknown ones and warns when an
answer has none; each answer shows its sources, linked to the NASA reports. The assistant does not compute numbers, does
not change any data or model, and is not used to produce the dashboard's results. Its behaviour on 10 test questions
(including questions outside the data, a prompt-injection attempt and a false premise) is recorded in
docs/ASSISTANT_EVAL.md. All other numbers in the dashboard come from deterministic code, not from AI.
Development: the code, analysis and texts of this project were written with Claude Code (Anthropic) under the direction
of the team member, who made every scientific and design decision (table above, docs/DECISIONS.md). No AI-generated
images or video.
